Privacy Policy

Enchanted Chess · last updated 26 July 2026

Enchanted Chess collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. Nothing you do in the game is sent anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

What the game stores, and where

Your progress is written to your own device's local browser storage and stays there. It is readable only by this app, is never transmitted, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. The keys are:

  • enchanted-chess:run — the run you are on: gold, mana, seats beaten, relics.
  • enchanted-chess:v2 — the board in progress, so a game survives being closed.
  • enchanted-chess:tallies — local win/loss counts shown on the Ledger screen.
  • enchanted-chess:bench — a local measurement of your device's speed, used to pick how long the opponent may think.

None of this contains personal information. It is game state: piece positions, numbers and flags. You can erase all of it at any time from Android's Settings → Apps → Enchanted Chess → Storage → Clear data.

Permissions

The app requests one Android permission, INTERNET. It is required because the game runs in a system WebView, which declares it. The campaign is entirely offline: the engine, the opponents and every screen are contained in the app, and the game is fully playable in aeroplane mode.

Children

The game is a chess variant with no chat, no user-generated content, no social features and no purchases. Since it collects no data at all, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version ever collects anything — online play against other people is the one feature that would require it — this page will be updated before that version is published, and the change will be described here rather than buried.
